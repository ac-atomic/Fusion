Common questions

How do I play?

Swipe to slide every tile. Two matching elements that touch fuse into the next one up: H + H makes He, He + He makes Li, and so on.

What is Fission mode?

Uranium sinks to the bottom and never fuses. Swipe a neutron down onto it to split it for big points. Push two uranium together for critical mass. Switch modes in Settings.

Will I lose my progress if I switch modes?

No. Classic and Fission each keep their own game in progress and their own best score.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Open Settings with the gear icon and use the Sound and Haptics toggles.

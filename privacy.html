Fusion / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

Fusion does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your scores, games in progress, discovered elements, and settings (such as sound, haptics, appearance, and palette) are saved only on your iPhone so the game remembers where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What I collect

Nothing. Fusion does not use third-party SDKs for tracking, advertising, or analytics, and it does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

Apple

If you've opted in to share data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Children

Fusion is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
